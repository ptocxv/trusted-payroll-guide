# Payroll Compass

Payroll Passport is a trusted knowledge assistant for payroll consultants. It does not simply search documents or generate an answer. It determines which information is reliable and applicable to a specific client, country, employee context and date.

For every question, Payroll Passport provides a clear answer supported by traceable sources, identifies outdated or conflicting information and explains why one source should be trusted over another. When the available knowledge is insufficient, it connects the consultant with the most relevant expert. Once the question is resolved, the validated response is converted into reusable organisational knowledge.

By creating this closed loop from question to evidence, expert validation and knowledge capture, Payroll Passport helps consultants answer customers faster, reduces the risk of using incorrect information and prevents critical payroll expertise from remaining trapped in documents, inboxes or individual colleagues’ experience.

We are not replacing payroll experts with AI. We are making payroll expertise traceable, verifiable and reusable.
